04 / Looking forward

The work I want next should feel useful, ambitious, and human.

I’m most interested in products where strong engineering can remove friction from something genuinely important—how people learn, work, make decisions, or find the information they need.

I want to keep growing across the full product lifecycle: understanding the problem, shaping the interface, building reliable systems, learning from users, and refining what ships. I’m especially excited by teams that value thoughtful craft, open collaboration, and continuous learning.
